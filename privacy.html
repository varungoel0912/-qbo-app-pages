Privacy Policy

This application is a private, internal tool built for and used exclusively by Silverman Medical (a membership-based medical practice). It connects to the practice's own QuickBooks Online company to read recurring invoice data for the sole purpose of internal cash-flow forecasting.

No data accessed by this application is shared, sold, or transmitted to any third party. Data is used only within the practice's internal operations. This application is not distributed publicly and is not available to other QuickBooks users or companies.
